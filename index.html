Habit Route Support

Habit Route is an offline habit tracker for ordinary routines and optional progressive routes.

Contact

For help, feedback or a privacy question, email don@unswrc.com.

Export your history

Open Settings, then choose Export CSV. Habit Route creates the file on your device and opens the iOS share sheet. The file goes only to the destination you choose.

Delete your data

Open Settings to clear completion history or delete all app data. You can also remove all locally stored app data by deleting Habit Route from your device.

Reminders

Habit Route asks for notification permission only when you enable a reminder. Reminders are scheduled locally and can be changed in the app or in iOS Settings.
