Habit Route Privacy Policy

Effective date: 30 August 2026

Summary

Habit Route is designed to work offline. The developer does not collect, receive, sell or share your personal data. The app has no account, advertising, analytics or tracking.

Data stored on your device

Habit Route stores the information you enter locally on your device. This can include habit names and notes, schedules, route items, reminder choices, completion history and app preferences.

Notifications

If you enable a reminder, Habit Route asks iOS to schedule that notification locally. The app does not send reminder information to the developer or to an app server.

CSV export and sharing

When you choose Export CSV, Habit Route creates an export locally and presents the iOS share sheet. Nothing is transmitted until you choose a destination. Any service or person you select receives the file under their own privacy terms.

Backups

Your device backup may include app data depending on your Apple and device settings. Those backups are provided and controlled by Apple; the Habit Route developer cannot access them.

Delete or export your data

In Settings, you can export completion history, clear completion history or delete all app data. Deleting the app also removes its locally stored data from the device, subject to any device backups you control.

Children

Habit Route is a general productivity app and is not directed to children. Because the developer collects no data, the app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If the app’s data practices change, this policy and the App Store privacy information will be updated before the changed practice is released.

Contact
